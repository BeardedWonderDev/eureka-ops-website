# Eureka Ops Homepage Copy Outline

## Purpose
This document turns the approved homepage strategy into a practical copy outline for the first live Eureka Ops website.

Primary goal:
- get a credible, high-trust site live quickly
- speak directly to founder-led home service and trade businesses
- position Eureka Ops as a practical AI workforce design firm, not a generic AI agency
- drive one clear action: **Request a Pilot Review**

Recommended launch structure:
- single-page homepage
- privacy page
- pilot review modal flow

---

## Core Positioning

### Primary audience
Founder-led blue-collar service businesses, especially:
- plumbers
- HVAC companies
- electricians
- painters
- roofers
- landscapers
- cleaning companies
- general contractors
- restoration and field service businesses

### Primary pain
- follow-up breaks when the business gets busy
- operations become reactive
- too much depends on the owner
- growth is capped by lack of execution capacity

### Offer framing
Eureka Ops designs custom AI workforce systems for service businesses that need more operational capacity without hiring a full department first.

### Primary CTA
**Request a Pilot Review**

### CTA behavior
The CTA should open a contract form wizard modal that:
1. collects contact information first
2. then loads a Go High Level appointment booking calendar via API integration

### Tone
- practical
- no-BS
- premium
- grounded
- slightly futuristic, but never hypey

### Founder posture
Founder-forward. Brian should be presented as an operator and systems-minded builder focused on how work actually gets done.

## 2. Problem Section

### Goal
Make the right owner feel understood.

### Recommended heading
**Most service businesses do not have a lead problem. They have an execution problem.**

### Recommended body direction
When the business gets busy, the important work starts slipping:
- leads do not get followed up consistently
- estimates sit too long
- customer communication becomes reactive
- scheduling and coordination get messy
- admin work piles up
- too much still depends on the owner remembering everything

The result is not just stress. It is stalled growth.

### Recommended supporting line
The issue is rarely ambition. The issue is lack of reliable execution capacity.

### Card / bullet ideas
- **Follow-up slips** — inquiries and estimates go cold when the team gets busy.
- **Coordination gets messy** — jobs, people, and customer communication fall out of sync.
- **Admin backs up** — important work gets delayed because no one owns it consistently.
- **The owner stays the bottleneck** — growth keeps routing through one person.

### Notes
This section should feel uncomfortably accurate in a good way. It is one of the most important trust-building sections on the page.

---

## 3. What Eureka Ops Does

### Goal
Explain the offer simply, clearly, and credibly.

### Recommended heading
**Custom AI workforce systems for the work that keeps falling through the cracks.**

### Recommended body direction
Eureka Ops helps service businesses build AI-supported operational capacity around the workflows that are limiting growth.

That can mean:
- one focused AI-supported role
- several specialized roles working together
- a small operational function built around a broken workflow

This is not:
- a generic chatbot
- a canned automation package
- software handed off for you to figure out on your own

This is:
- custom workforce design
- workflow-first implementation
- practical deployment
- ongoing refinement and management

### Recommended 3-column structure
- **Diagnose the bottleneck** — identify the workflow creating the most drag.
- **Design the workforce** — create the right AI-supported role, system, or micro-team around it.
- **Deploy and refine** — implement the pilot, monitor it, and improve it over time.

### Notes
Keep this section grounded. The promise is targeted operational capacity, not magical autonomy.

---

## 4. Example Workflow Use Cases

### Goal
Make the offer concrete for blue-collar service businesses.

### Recommended heading
**Start with the workflow that is creating the most drag.**

### Recommended intro
The best pilots start with one recurring workflow that is already costing the business time, missed revenue, or operational consistency.

### Recommended use case cards
- **Lead Follow-Up**  
  Make sure new inquiries do not go cold when the team gets busy.

- **Estimate Follow-Up**  
  Stay on top of open quotes and improve close rates without relying on memory.

- **Customer Communication**  
  Keep customers informed with more consistent reminders, updates, and post-job follow-through.

- **Scheduling & Coordination Support**  
  Reduce communication breakdowns between the office, the field, and the customer.

- **Review & Reputation Follow-Up**  
  Turn completed work into more reviews and stronger local trust.

- **Admin Execution**  
  Keep recurring operational tasks from piling up or getting forgotten.

- **Inquiry Intake & Routing**  
  Organize inbound requests faster and route them to the right next step.

- **Post-Job Reactivation**  
  Re-engage past customers for follow-up services, seasonal work, or maintenance reminders.

### Notes
This section should feel specific to home service and trade businesses without sounding like a narrow gimmick page.

## 5. How the Pilot Works

### Goal
Make the engagement feel practical, bounded, and safe.

### Recommended heading
**Start with a focused pilot, not a giant overhaul.**

### Recommended intro
We do not begin by trying to redesign your whole business. We start with one workflow that is creating real drag, then build a focused pilot around it.

### Recommended 4-step flow
1. **Pilot Review**  
   We identify the workflow creating the most drag in your business.

2. **Workforce Design**  
   We design the right AI-supported role, system, or micro-team around that workflow.

3. **Deployment**  
   We build and implement the pilot inside the real operating environment.

4. **Refinement**  
   We monitor, improve, and expand it if the pilot proves valuable.

### Recommended supporting line
The goal is to create measurable operational leverage quickly, without overbuilding.

### Notes
This section lowers resistance. It should communicate scope, structure, and practicality.

---

## 6. Why This Instead of Hiring First

### Goal
Justify the category and support the value framing.

### Recommended heading
**You may need the function before you can justify the hires.**

### Recommended body direction
Many service businesses know they need better follow-up, stronger coordination, or more operational support. But hiring a full department is expensive, slow, and often too blunt an answer to a fragmented problem.

A focused AI workforce pilot can help create targeted execution capacity around the exact workflow that is currently limiting growth.

### Recommended comparison structure
#### Traditional path
- hire for one or more support roles
- salary, taxes, training, and management overhead
- slower ramp-up
- harder to shape around fragmented workflow gaps

#### Eureka Ops path
- identify the exact bottleneck first
- build targeted AI-supported capacity around that workflow
- deploy faster than staffing a whole function
- lower ongoing cost than building full headcount for the same narrow problem
- refine and improve over time

### Pricing/value guidance
Use generalized illustrative framing only. Do not publish hard package pricing on the homepage.

Suggested positioning lines:
- For many businesses, even one additional full-time hire can cost far more annually than a focused pilot and ongoing management.
- The goal is not to replace every employee. The goal is to create targeted execution capacity where the business is currently constrained.

### Notes
This section should support premium value without turning the homepage into a pricing page.

## 9. FAQ

### Goal
Resolve skepticism and answer the highest-probability objections.

### Recommended heading
**Questions owners usually ask before they reach out**

### Recommended FAQs
#### Is this just a chatbot or automation setup?
No. Eureka Ops designs custom AI workforce systems around real workflows inside the business. The goal is operational capacity, not a generic bot.

#### What kinds of businesses are the best fit?
Founder-led home service and trade businesses with recurring operational friction, inconsistent follow-up, and obvious execution bottlenecks.

#### Do I need to replace my team?
No. The goal is to add capacity, consistency, and leverage — not blindly replace people.

#### How do you decide what to build first?
We start with the workflow creating the most drag, missed opportunity, or owner dependency in the business.

#### What does a pilot include?
A pilot starts with review and diagnosis, then moves into design, implementation, and early refinement around one bounded workflow.

#### Do you only work with home service and trade businesses?
That is the primary focus for the first version of the site because the operational patterns are clear and the value is easy to identify.

#### Why not just hire someone?
Sometimes hiring is the right move. But when the bottleneck is fragmented, repetitive, and operational, a focused AI-supported system can often be deployed faster and more cost-effectively.

### Notes
Keep answers short, direct, and grounded.
